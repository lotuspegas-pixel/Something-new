How it works

Two of your own devices, one minute of setup. No app, no account, nothing stored.

1
Start the baby unit

Open babyphone.online on the device that stays with the baby — a spare phone, tablet or laptop — and tap Start baby unit. Allow camera and microphone. You'll see a 6-character room code and a QR code.

2
Start the parent unit

Open babyphone.online on your own phone and tap Connect as parent.

3
Connect

Type the room code or scan the QR. That's it — you see and hear your baby live, and can talk back, play a lullaby, or turn on the night light from your side.

What you get

  • Live video and sound with low delay, also on 4G/5G
  • Talk back — your voice on the baby unit at the tap of a button
  • Lullabies and your own MP3 playlist, played on the baby device
  • Cry alert with adjustable sensitivity, plus an event log
  • Night light, sleep timer, privacy shade (sound without video)
  • Battery status of the baby device on your screen
  • Interface in 30 languages

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an app?
No. Everything runs in the browser on both devices.
Is my baby's video stored anywhere?
No. Video and audio stream directly between your own two devices (peer-to-peer via WebRTC), encrypted in transit. Nothing is recorded or seen by a server. Details in the privacy policy.
Does it work when the devices are on different networks?
Yes — wifi at home, 4G/5G on the go. When a direct path is impossible, an encrypted relay forwards the stream without being able to view it.
What does it cost?
The core baby monitor is free, with no account required.
What do I need?
Two devices with a modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). The baby unit needs a camera and microphone; keep it on a charger.
Safety: BabyPhone.online is an aid, not a replacement for in-person supervision of your baby. Connections depend on your devices and networks and can fail.
