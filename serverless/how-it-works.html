How it works

Two of your own devices, one minute of setup. No app, no account, nothing stored.

1
Start the pet cam

Open petcam.online on the device that stays with your pet — a spare phone, tablet or laptop — and tap Start pet cam. Allow camera and microphone. You'll see a 6-character room code and a QR code.

2
Start the owner unit

Open petcam.online on your own phone and tap Connect as owner.

3
Connect

Type the room code or scan the QR. That's it — you see and hear your pet live, and can talk back, play a comfort sound, or turn on the night light from your side.

Open PetCam.online

What you get

  • Live video and sound with low delay, also on 4G/5G
  • Talk back — your voice on the pet cam at the tap of a button
  • Comfort sounds and your own MP3 playlist, played on the pet cam device
  • Sound alert for barking, whining or other noise, with adjustable sensitivity, plus an event log
  • Night light, rest timer, privacy shade (sound without video)
  • Battery status of the pet cam device on your screen
  • Interface in 30 languages

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an app?
No. Everything runs in the browser on both devices.
Is my pet's video stored anywhere?
No. Video and audio stream directly between your own two devices (peer-to-peer via WebRTC), encrypted in transit. Nothing is recorded or seen by a server. Details in the privacy policy.
Does it work when the devices are on different networks?
Yes — wifi at home, 4G/5G on the go. When a direct path is impossible, an encrypted relay forwards the stream without being able to view it.
What does it cost?
The core pet monitor is free, with no account required.
What do I need?
Two devices with a modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). The pet cam needs a camera and microphone; keep it on a charger.
Safety: PetCam.online is an aid, not a replacement for in-person supervision of your pet. Connections depend on your devices and networks and can fail.
