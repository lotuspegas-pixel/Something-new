Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 8 July 2026 · Prepared in line with the European Accessibility Act

Our commitment

PetCam.online is built for busy pet owners — often operating it one-handed, in the dark, at 3 a.m. Accessibility is core to that: the app should work with a screen reader, with a keyboard, with reduced vision, and with shaky hands.

Conformance target

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. Measures currently in place include:

  • Accessible names (aria-label) on all icon-only controls (microphone, camera, fullscreen, settings, and monitor buttons).
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators on all interactive elements.
  • Large touch targets and high-contrast text on the dark interface.
  • Status information (connection, battery, sound level) conveyed as text, not color alone.
  • Support for browser zoom up to 200% without loss of function.

Known limitations

  • The live audio level meter is a visual indicator; sound events are also logged as text in the event log, but real-time non-visual alerting is limited to the (audible) sound alert.
  • The QR scanner requires a camera; the room code can always be typed instead.
  • Some of the 30 interface languages have partially translated interface strings; untranslated strings appear in English.

Feedback and contact

If you hit an accessibility barrier, tell us: [contact email], subject "Accessibility". We aim to respond within 5 business days and to fix confirmed barriers in the next release. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the supervisory authority in your EU member state.
