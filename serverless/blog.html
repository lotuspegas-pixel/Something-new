Two phones, one free baby monitor: everything BabyPhone.online can do

No box to buy, no app to install, no account. Open the site on two devices you already own and you have a private baby monitor with live video and sound — at home or on holiday.

4 min readUpdated July 2026

A traditional baby monitor is one more gadget to buy, charge and pack. BabyPhone.online takes a different route: it turns devices you already have into the camera and the monitor, right in the browser. Here is why that is so handy — and what every button does.

Give an old phone or tablet a second life

That drawer phone from two upgrades ago, or a tablet the kids barely use — either one becomes the baby camera. Prop it up by the crib, open babyphone.online, tap “Baby unit”, and it streams live video and sound to your own phone. No SIM card needed: home wifi is enough. A real baby camera, without buying — or throwing away — anything.

Made for holidays and nights away

This is where it shines. On holiday you do not want to pack a bulky monitor with its own short-range signal. With BabyPhone.online you simply use the two phones you already carry: leave one in the hotel room or tent with the baby, take the other to the terrace or the room next door. Because the connection runs over the internet (wifi or 4G/5G), the range is not limited to a single building and you are not tied to a base-station signal. Two phones, and you are set — anywhere.

Connected in under a minute

Pairing is deliberately simple, so you can do it half-asleep at 3 a.m.

  1. On the baby’s device, tap “Baby unit”. It shows a 6-character code and a QR code.
  2. On your own phone, tap “Parent unit”.
  3. Type the code or scan the QR — you instantly see and hear your baby.

Private by design

Video and sound travel directly between your two devices, peer-to-peer and encrypted in transit. Nothing is recorded, and no server can watch along. No account, no ads, no tracking — just your own devices talking to each other.

Works on the devices you already own

Any phone, tablet or laptop with a recent browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) works — including older models, as long as the browser is reasonably up to date. One device needs a camera and microphone for the baby side; keep it on its charger and you are set for the whole night.

How a baby monitor over wifi actually works

A classic baby monitor is a pair of short-range radios: the camera unit transmits, the parent unit receives, and a thick wall ends the conversation. A wifi baby monitor sends video and sound as data over the internet instead. BabyPhone.online uses WebRTC — the same technology your browser uses for video calls — so the two devices open a direct, encrypted link to each other. The room code only helps them find each other at the start, and an encrypted relay steps in solely when your network blocks a direct path; neither can see or hear the stream. The practical difference: range stops being measured in metres, but both devices do need to be online.

Baby monitor app or a dedicated baby monitor?

Neither wins outright — they fail in different ways. An honest comparison.

  • RangeA dedicated monitor works anywhere within its radio range and needs no internet at all. An app has effectively unlimited range, but both devices have to be online.
  • CostA video monitor costs real money and becomes e-waste when your child outgrows it. This costs nothing and reuses hardware you already own.
  • TravelA base station, a camera and two chargers fill half a bag. Two phones you were carrying anyway fill none.
  • ReliabilityA dedicated unit does one job, with no OS update or incoming call in the way. A phone needs care: keep it on the charger, screen awake, other apps closed.
  • PrivacyCloud cameras keep footage on a vendor's servers; cheap analogue monitors can be picked up by a receiver nearby. A peer-to-peer link avoids both — but you decide who gets the room code.
  • Night visionDedicated units have infrared LEDs; a phone camera cannot do that. In a pitch-dark room you will hear everything and see little — the built-in night light helps.

Frequently asked questions

  • Is a baby monitor app safe?It depends on where the video goes and who can join. Here the stream runs straight between your two devices, encrypted in transit, so there is no cloud account to break into and no footage on someone else's server. The baby unit does not auto-answer either: a device that scanned the QR carries a one-time token, and anyone who typed the code by hand has to be approved on the baby device's own screen.
  • Does a baby monitor work without wifi?This one needs internet on both devices — but not wifi specifically, and not the same network: the baby device can be on wifi while you are on 4G or 5G. With no internet at all on either side the two devices cannot find each other, and a classic radio monitor is the better tool.
  • What does it cost?Nothing. Every feature is free, there is no paid tier and no account, and the site carries no ads and no trackers.
  • Can I use an old phone?Yes, and that is the idea. Any spare phone or tablet with a camera, a microphone and an up-to-date browser will do, with or without a SIM card. Keep it plugged in — streaming video all night empties a battery.
  • Do I need to install anything?No. Both devices just open the website. There is nothing to download, nothing to sign up for and nothing to configure.
  • How many people can watch at once?A second viewer can join with the same room code, but the baby unit asks for approval on its own screen first — so nobody watches without someone in the room agreeing.
