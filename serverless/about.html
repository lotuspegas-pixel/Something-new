About BabyPhone.online

Last updated: 2 November 2026

What it is

BabyPhone.online is a free baby monitor that runs in the browser. Two devices you already own — a spare phone or tablet for the baby’s room and the phone in your pocket — become the baby unit and the parent unit, with live video and sound. There is no app to install and no account to create.

Who runs it

BabyPhone.online is operated by Arcana Synthetica, based in Harlingen, the Netherlands (Chamber of Commerce number 42115786). The lullabies and other audio files on the site are made by Arcana Synthetica. Full operator and contact details are on the contact page.

What we will and will not do

  • Free, in full. Every feature is free. There is no paid tier and no subscription.
  • No advertising, no tracking. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and shows no ads. The only thing kept on your device is your own language choice.
  • Nothing is stored. Video and sound go directly between your two devices, encrypted, and are not recorded on a server. Recordings you choose to make are saved on your own device.
  • Only people with the code get in. Pairing needs the room code or the QR code, and a device that typed the code by hand must be approved on the baby unit’s own screen.
  • Available in 30 languages, on phones, tablets and computers with a reasonably current browser.

What it is not

It is an aid, not a replacement for supervising your baby in person. It needs an internet connection on both devices, and it has no infrared night vision. If the baby unit is locked or the browser is sent to the background, a phone may pause the page; the setup guide explains how to avoid that.

How it is paid for

The site is free to use. People who want to help keep it free and ad-free can make an optional donation using the “Support us” button on the home page. Nothing is held back from people who do not donate.
