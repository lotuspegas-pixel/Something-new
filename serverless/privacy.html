Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026 · Applies to babyphone.online

The short version: your baby's video and audio stream directly between your own two devices (peer-to-peer, via WebRTC). The stream is never stored, never recorded by us, and never passes through or is seen by our servers. There are no accounts, no logins, no visitor analytics and no advertising, and the service is completely free.

1. Who we are

BabyPhone.online is operated by Arcana Synthetica, Harlingen, The Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 42115786 ("we"). We are the data controller for the processing described in this policy. Contact: info@babyphone.online. We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer, so questions go straight to that address.

2. What BabyPhone.online does

BabyPhone.online turns two of your own devices into a baby monitor inside the browser. One device (the baby unit) captures camera and microphone; the other (the parent unit) shows the live stream. The connection between the two devices is made with WebRTC and is encrypted in transit (DTLS-SRTP). The service is free of charge; there is nothing to buy, no account and no subscription.

3. What we process — and what we do not

  • Video and audio: streamed directly from your baby unit to your parent unit. We do not receive, store, record, or have any access to this stream. If you use the recording feature, the recording is written to the device you are recording on and is never uploaded to us.
  • Room code: a random 6-character code used to pair your two devices. It identifies a session, not a person, and stops working when you close the app. It passes through the pairing broker described in section 5.
  • Language preference: when — and only when — you pick a language yourself, your browser stores that choice on your own device under the name babyfoon.lang. It contains nothing but a language code such as nl, it never leaves your device, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No accounts, no profiles: we do not ask for your name, email address, or any registration to use the monitor.
  • No visitor analytics: we do not use Google Analytics or any other statistics or tracking tool of our own.
  • No selling, no profiling: we do not sell or trade personal data, and we take no automated decisions about you.

4. Cookies and what is stored on your device

This website sets no cookies at all — not our own, and none from anyone else. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking script on any page.

The only thing ever written to your device is your language preference, stored in your browser under babyfoon.lang as a two-letter code, and only once you actively pick a language. It never leaves your device. Under Dutch law (Article 11.7a of the Telecommunications Act) that counts as strictly necessary for a function you asked for, so it needs no consent — it is simply your own setting, and clearing your browser storage removes it.

Because nothing else is placed or read on your device, this site needs no cookie banner and asks you for no consent.

5. Connection helpers (third parties)

To let your two devices find each other, the app uses a small set of connection helpers. These services help establish the connection; none of them can watch or listen to your baby:

  • Pairing broker — PeerJS Cloud (0.peerjs.com): exchanges the technical connection details ("signalling") between your two devices when you enter the room code. It sees your IP address and your room code for as long as the app is open. It never sees video or audio.
  • STUN — Google (stun.l.google.com): tells your device what its public IP address is, so the two devices can describe themselves to each other. It only sees an IP address.
  • TURN — the Open Relay Project, operated by Metered (openrelay.metered.ca): a fallback used only when a direct connection is impossible (for example on some mobile networks). It sees your IP address and forwards the encrypted stream; it holds no decryption key and cannot view or record it.

Legal basis: performing the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Nothing here is stored beyond the length of your session, and none of it is used for advertising.

6. Hosting and server logs

The website is served by Hostinger. Like every web server, it writes standard access logs — your IP address, the time, the page requested, the response code and your browser type — plus error logs for failed requests. These are used only to keep the site running and to defend against abuse, never to build a profile of you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Hostinger processes these logs for us as a processor, under its Data Processing Addendum. In our hosting panel these logs can be viewed for up to the last 7 days, which is the longest window the provider makes available to us; we do not copy, export or store them anywhere else.

7. Contact form

If you use the contact form on our contact page, the name, email address, optional phone number and message you type are sent by email to info@babyphone.online and are not stored on this website or in any database. We use them for one purpose only: to answer you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in replying to your question (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Your message is delivered and kept in our mailbox by our email provider, who handles it on our instructions and for no other purpose. We keep the resulting correspondence for as long as needed to handle your question and then delete it, at the latest 12 months after our last reply.

To keep the form from being abused for spam, the server briefly keeps a one-way hash of your IP address (not the IP itself, and not reversible) together with a timestamp, purely to count submissions. That record expires automatically after one hour. Nothing else about the submission is written to disk.

8. Children

BabyPhone.online exists to watch a baby, so let us be plain about it. The image and the sound of your child are personal data of your child. They are captured by your device and shown on your other device, and they travel between the two encrypted. We never receive them, never store them and cannot play them back — there is no server of ours in the middle of that stream, and we could not hand the footage to anyone even if we were asked.

You, the parent or legal guardian, decide when the camera is on, who gets the room code and whether to record. Keep the room code to yourself: while your session is open, anyone who has it can connect. If you record, the file sits on your own device and it is yours to keep or delete.

The service is meant to be set up and used by adults. It is not aimed at children as users, we never knowingly collect personal data directly from a child, and we never ask any visitor for their age.

9. Transfers outside the EU

Some of the connection helpers above are based in, or route traffic through, the United States: Google’s public STUN server, the PeerJS Cloud pairing broker, and the Open Relay TURN service. Where they receive data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the safeguards those providers publish — the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Your baby's video and audio are not part of any of these transfers.

10. Retention

We store nothing about your monitoring sessions — no room codes, no session records, no stream — so there is nothing there to retain or delete. The only things with a lifetime are: the spam-protection hash from the contact form (one hour), email correspondence (deleted at the latest 12 months after our last reply), and hosting access logs (viewable for up to 7 days in our hosting panel).

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask us for a copy of your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to have its use restricted, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to receive it in a portable form.

In practice we hold almost nothing about you: the only data we can look up is email you have sent us. To exercise any right, email info@babyphone.online with the subject "Privacy request" and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl), or with the supervisory authority in your own EU country.

12. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be announced in the app.
